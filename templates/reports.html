Attendance Calculation

  • P means Present.
  • A means Absent.
  • - means no attendance was recorded for that period.
  • Leisure periods are not counted.
  • If no class was taken, no attendance record is created, so that period is not counted.
  • Adjusted classes count when attendance is recorded.
  • Attendance percentage = Present ÷ Conducted × 100.
  • Attendance below 75% is marked as Low Attendance.
